The Industry's AI Is a Black Box. Ours Comes With X-Ray Vision.

The #1 fear every dealer has about AI call handling: the AI silently kills leads and nobody knows. Here's how other vendors leave you blind — and how RoyceAI is different.

❌ The black box

The AI decides who's a buyer and who isn't. Nobody sees the rejects. Low activity could mean a slow month — or the AI quietly filtering out buyers a human would have closed. You'll never know which.

✅ X-ray vision

Every single call and lead is recorded with a full transcript. You get a weekly audit report: inbound → AI-handled → transferred → disqualified → followed up. If over-disqualification shows up, we adjust the threshold. You see everything.

❌ Nobody's watching

The AI runs 24/7 and nobody audits it. If it's doing a bad job, the only signal is a quiet pipeline — and by then the damage is done.

✅ Weekly 10-call audit built in

A random sample of AI-handled calls is reviewed by a human every week. If the AI messed up, we fix the rules. No blind spots, ever.

❌ No close-rate accountability

Vendors sell you "answered calls." They don't show you the funnel — calls answered → appointments booked → appointments kept → sold. If conversion dips, you find out in a quarter.

✅ Full funnel, weekly

You see calls answered → appointments booked → kept → sold, plus close rate by lead source. If the AI's conversion dips, you know within the week — not the quarter.
