How to read these lists

In network

Read off the plan's own finder for that city's ZIP on the network your dental plan runs on. This is the payer's record, not an inference.

Confirm network

On the city's public quality list, but not verified against the payer — the vision plan's finder withholds its list until a member signs in. One call to the office, or one look at the finder, settles it.

Scores

Public quality scores from expert-inspection directories that weigh reviews, history, price and trust. A dash means no published score — not a bad score.
