Inside the JLR systems — CRM, process, vehicle location

At Jaguar Land Rover Newport Beach I do not just carry a client list. I am wired into the systems the store runs on, and everything a client hears from me comes out of them:

  • CRM follow-up. Every client relationship lives in eLead CRM — first contact, follow-up cadence, task queues, notes and outcomes. I work my own pipeline daily, so nothing goes cold and no follow-up is dropped.
  • Process and update management. I own the record as the deal moves: order and allocation updates, delivery scheduling, status changes, and the internal handoffs between sales, F&I, service and management. The system stays current because I keep it current.
  • Vehicle location. I track units through Dealergard / Stargard GPS and inventory status — where the car actually is, in transit, on the lot, or at the shop — so a client gets an answer, not a guess.
  • Client communications. All three feed the same thing: what I tell a client on a call, in a text, or in person is pulled from the live system record — accurate status, honest timing, no false expectations. That is how you keep a client after the sale.
  • Why it matters here. The same discipline is what I bring to a deployment: understand the system of record, keep it true, and communicate from it — to the customer, the field team and the program office.
