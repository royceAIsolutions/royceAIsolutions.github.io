How the score works

  • Value vs. new (up to 58): the asking price measured against the current street price of that exact model line, not the seller's "retail" claim. At 65% of new you get full points; every point above that costs.
  • Brand (up to 12): a known brand with real parts and battery support scores higher than an unbranded import.
  • Local (up to 12): Irvine to 5 miles is full credit, decaying to 0 past 40 miles.
  • Model year (up to 6) and listing text (±): recent years score up; "needs battery", "no charger", "salvage" score down; owner history and receipts score up.
  • The battery is the bike. Ask for miles and charge cycles, charge it to full before paying, and check for swelling. Replacement packs run several hundred to over a thousand dollars.
  • Legal line: Class 1/2 assist to 20 mph, Class 3 to 28 mph - 28 mph is the ceiling. Anything shipped at 32+ mph (or dual-motor, 60V+) is a motor vehicle, not an e-bike: licence, registration, no bike lanes. Those listings are filtered out.
  • Range is two numbers, never one. Seller says is what the listing claims at full charge; Maker spec is the manufacturer's published figure for that model line at full charge. When a seller states none, only the spec is shown; when no maker figure is published for the model, the card says so rather than guessing.
  • Filters that removed 5 listings: parts and accessories, scam and financing spam, knockoff "73 style" clones, storefront reseller boilerplate, and machines over the e-bike speed ceiling.
