How the SBA 7(a) math works

Every deal below is under $500K, which fits the SBA 7(a) sweet spot: 90% financing, 10% down, up to 10 years. We compute the debt service and DSCR on the seller-reported cash flow so you can see the real picture before you call a broker.

1. What you put down

  • 10% down on the purchase price (SBA 7(a) standard)
  • A $300K business needs ~$30K down + closing costs
  • No collateral needed for loans under $500K — SBA 90% guarantee

2. What the loan costs

  • Rates roughly 7%–9%, fixed via SBIC lending
  • Term up to 10 years for business acquisition
  • $300K loan ≈ ~$3,100/mo debt service

3. The 1.25x rule

  • Lenders want DSCR ≥ 1.25: cash flow at least 1.25× the annual loan payment
  • Quick test: cash flow ÷ price ≥ 10.5%
  • We flag every deal's DSCR on the card — green = lender-comfortable
