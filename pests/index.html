Kitchen Recipes

Three spray mixes you can make in 10 minutes.

  • 🌶️
    Pepper Spray
    2-3 tbsp cayenne or chili powder per gallon of water. Mix, let sit 10 minutes, dump on patio edges and cracks. Strongest natural deterrent. Reapply after rain.
  • 🧄
    Garlic Water
    5-6 crushed garlic cloves soaked in water overnight. Strain and dump on patio. Milder than pepper but effective. Lasts a few days.
  • 🧪
    Vinegar Spray
    50/50 white vinegar and water. Spray around edges and cracks. Deters pests and kills weeds. Safe for plants in small amounts.

Pleasant Solutions

Things that smell amazing to you but pests absolutely hate.

  • 🌿
    Peppermint Oil
    Smells incredible. Works on ants, spiders, roaches, and rodents. One bottle covers 4 pests. Put on cotton balls, spray entry points, or diffuse.
  • 🌸
    Lavender / Eucalyptus
    Smells like a spa. Spiders, flies, and rodents avoid it. Essential oil spray around windows and doors.
  • 🍪
    Cinnamon
    Smells like baking cookies. Ants and silverfish wont cross it. Sprinkle powder at entry points or place sticks in drawers.
  • 🌱
    Potted Mint / Lemongrass
    Looks great on the patio. Repels lizards, flies, and mosquitoes year-round. Smells fresh every time you walk by.
  • 🌲
    Cedar Oil
    Smells like a forest. Silverfish and moths hate it. Spray in closets and drawers to protect clothes naturally.
  • 🕯️
    Cloves / Bay Leaves
    Smells like the holidays. Roaches and silverfish stay away. Whole cloves in sachets or bay leaves in cabinets.
