Privacy Policy

Royce AI Tutor · Last updated 5 November 2026

This policy covers the Royce AI Tutor iOS app and the student dashboard at royceai.com/tutor/dashboard. It explains exactly what we collect, why, where it goes, and how to get it deleted. We do not sell data, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Student first and last nameWhen a student or tutor starts a quizTo attach quiz results to the right student and let them sign back in to their dashboard
Quiz answers and scores (question, chosen answer, correctness, subject, timestamp)During every quizTo score the quiz, show progress, and pick appropriate difficulty next time
Grade level and subject listOn the account recordTo serve age- and course-appropriate questions
Name, email, optional phone, and messageOnly if the contact form is usedTo reply to support and tutoring enquiries

We do not collect precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, health data, advertising identifiers, or browsing history. The app does not request those permissions.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs inside the app; we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No sale or rental of personal information to anyone.
  • No profiling for marketing and no automated decisions with legal effect.

Where the data goes

Quiz and profile data is sent over HTTPS to our own API (chat-api-tawny-zeta.vercel.app) and stored in our private data store on GitHub. Model-generated quiz questions are produced by an AI provider acting as our processor; requests include the quiz subject, the student's grade level, and quiz progress, and are not used to build advertising profiles. Hosting and model processing are the only third parties involved.

How long we keep it

  • Quiz history: kept while the student's account is active, capped at 500 sessions per student, newest kept.
  • Contact enquiries: kept until the enquiry is resolved, then up to 12 months for our records.
  • Deletion: when you ask us to delete an account, we remove the profile and quiz history within 30 days.

Children and student privacy

Royce AI Tutor is a study tool for school-age students and is intended for use by students aged 13 and older, or by younger students whose account is set up and supervised by a parent, guardian, or tutor. We ask for a name and grade only — never a school ID, address, or birth date. We do not serve ads to any user, we do not create behavioural profiles of children, and we do not allow third-party tracking of children. A parent or guardian can request review or deletion of a child's data at any time using the contact below.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to the data store is limited to the operator's credentials, and the student roster is never embedded in a public page. No system is perfect; if we ever become aware of a breach affecting student data, we will notify affected families.

Your choices

  • Access or correction: ask us for a copy of a student's record, or ask us to fix a name or grade.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete the account and its quiz history.
  • Contact form data: ask us to delete a support enquiry.
  • No account needed to browse: the public pages at royceai.com do not require personal information.

Website analytics

Pages on royceai.com (not the iOS app) use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It sets cookies and records page views, approximate location derived from IP, and device type. It is not used to identify individual students and is not connected to quiz records. This privacy page and the support page do not load analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it on this page for at least 30 days.
