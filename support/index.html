Signing in

The dashboard at royceai.com/tutor/dashboard asks for the student's first and last name exactly as enrolled — for example Carter and Koh. There is no password; the account is matched to the name on the tutoring record. If a name doesn't match, email us and we'll fix the spelling on the roster.

How the quizzes work

  • Each quiz is 33 questions, one at a time, four options each.
  • 80% is the pass mark — 27 of 33.
  • Questions follow the student's own courses and grade level, and get harder as accuracy improves.
  • Finishing a quiz saves the score to the dashboard scoreboard and quiz history.
  • Quitting early keeps the questions you answered but does not record a session.

My score didn't save

Scores are written when the quiz reaches the end and you answer the final question. If the app is closed before that, the session is dropped — that's intentional, so a half-finished quiz can't be mistaken for a real result. Re-run the quiz and let it finish; if a saved score still doesn't appear on the dashboard within a minute, email us with the subject and approximate time.

Offline use

The app carries an on-device question bank, so practice works without a connection. Scores and new AI-generated questions need a connection; anything taken offline is not uploaded later.

Which grade levels are supported

The iOS app ships with 9th grade material (Integrated Math 1, English 9, Biology, World History, Spanish 1, Critical Thinking). The web dashboard covers a wider spread — elementary through AP coursework — because it serves each enrolled student's own courses. If your student is in a different grade and you want the app aligned to their courses, email us.

Privacy and removing data

We collect a name, grade, and quiz results — nothing else. No ads, no tracking. To see or delete a student's data, email us and we will remove it within 30 days. Full details: Privacy Policy.

Purchases and billing

The app itself is free. Tutoring plans are sold separately through Royce AI Solutions, not through in-app purchase, so nothing in the app can charge you. Billing questions: email us.
