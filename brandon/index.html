Brandon Muzik
Founder & President, AmaTone

Brandon Muzik founded and leads AmaTone, a mobile app that analyzes the unique patterns in a person’s voice to produce real-time wellness indicators — digestion, hydration, sleep, stress and energy — from a 30-second recording, with no wearable required.

What AmaTone does

AmaTone’s proposition is that the voice carries biomarkers — subtle frequencies and tonal variations that reflect the body’s internal state. Record thirty seconds of speech and the app returns a read on five indicators plus recommendations, in real time, on iOS and Android.

The company positions the product for four audiences: everyday users, athletes, corporate wellness programs, and gym members. The stated aim is a clinically validated product that stays accessible and affordable — the alternative it replaces being an expensive wearable.
