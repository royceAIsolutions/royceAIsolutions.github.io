What RoyceAI puts
into AmaTone

RoyceAI powers AmaTone. We are not a vendor line item and not a subscription — we bring the engineering capacity, the AI systems and the automation that turn a voice-reading app into a company that can be funded, sold to employers and defended. Your voice science stays yours. We build everything around it.

What RoyceAI contributes

No subscription. The three things AmaTone needs come first — translation, funding and the IP estate — then the layers that make them work.

01

We take the product into the languages its next market speaks

English-only caps the reach: the same reading in Korean or Chinese reaches users, clinics and partners who never see it otherwise. We already run our own company in three languages — the entire royceai.com site, interface text, titles and meta included, is translated by the engine we built and maintain. That is the layer that carries AmaTone's app strings, store listing, site and investor material into the next market, and keeps them in step every time the English changes.

Opens the marketWe contribute Translation + localizationYou get Non-English users and buyers
02

We prepare the company to be funded

The raise is underwritten by the estate, not by the story, so we lead with what is issued and what is still open. Funders and acquirers do not buy a product — they buy a defensible asset: structure, documentation, IP standing, reporting, and the ability to answer diligence without scrambling. We assemble the operational spine that makes a raise or an exit a conversation about your numbers instead of your paperwork.

Diligence-readyWe contribute Operational buildYou get Raise and exit readiness
03

We manage the patent and trademark estate, filing by filing

An international portfolio is a set of expiring clocks. A missed response or an unfiled foreign priority does not just cost a filing fee — it narrows the estate a funder or acquirer is underwriting. We run the deadline desk: every jurisdiction, every basis, every response owed, tracked and escalated before it goes late. The same desk carries every basis that holds a patent position — convention priority and PCT national phase — beside the trademark bases already on file.

Defends the valuationWe contribute Automated monitoringYou get Protects the asset base
04

We turn readings into a product people open daily

AmaTone returns five indicators. That is data. What a user keeps paying for is the instruction: what changed in my voice, what it means, and the one thing to do today. We build the reasoning layer that turns each reading into plain-language guidance — so the app stops being a dashboard checked once and becomes the daily habit it was designed to be.

Turns data into a habitWe contribute Engineering + AI systemsYou get Retention and daily engagement
05

We build the accredited layer your buyers require

A corporate wellness buyer, a clinic or an insurer cannot adopt a black box. They need to see what the number means, how it was produced and whether it holds up over time. We build the validation, reporting and audit trail around your existing readings — the layer that lets a skeptical buyer say yes. That layer is the missing asset between your science and your revenue.

Unlocks B2B revenueWe contribute Systems + evidence infrastructureYou get Enterprise credibility
06

We give the company a front door

You sell to employers, programs and gyms — and those decisions start with a call. Today every inbound is handled by a founder who is also running product, IP and partnerships. We put an always-answering front desk on it: every inquiry captured, qualified and booked, with you seeing only the ones ready to talk.

Founder time backWe contribute AutomationYou get Zero lost inbound
