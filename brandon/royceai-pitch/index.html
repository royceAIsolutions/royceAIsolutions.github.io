What RoyceAI can do
for AmaTone

AmaTone already has the hard part: a working product and a clinical story. RoyceAI supplies the layer between the voice reading and the person acting on it, and the front door that turns corporate interest into booked demos.

Four things we can build for you

Ordered by how fast each one can happen, not by size.

01

24/7 AI Receptionist for the B2B motion

An AI phone line that answers every corporate-wellness, clinic and gym inquiry instantly, day or night. It qualifies the caller, books the demo into your calendar, and hands a warmed lead to a human. Your product is sold to employers and programs, and those buyers call during business hours you cannot staff alone.

Fastest first yes$3,000/mo per lineLive product, already in production for dealership and medical clients.
02

The explanation layer

An AI assistant that turns each reading into plain language: what changed in the voice, what it likely indicates, and the next action. This is the difference between a dashboard a user checks once and a product they open daily, and it is what a clinician needs before a corporate buyer will sign.

CredibilityScoped buildPositioned as evidence infrastructure: traceable, reviewable, defensible.
03

Trend detection and corporate reporting

The app already collects voice samples. The missing layer is what an employer pays for: longitudinal trends, a nudge when a user's pattern shifts, and a wellness report generated automatically each period. Passive collection becomes a recurring deliverable.

Recurring valueScoped buildTurns the B2B promise into a reportable outcome.
04

Speed to shipped

You have already run roughly 1,500 hours of development to reach 70+ testers. A focused AI build compresses the measurement-to-recommendation loop from quarters into weeks, using infrastructure that is already running rather than started from zero.

MomentumProjectBuilds on proven delivery, not a rewrite of your product.

Why this fits AmaTone

  • Your buyerCorporate wellness programs, clinics, gyms
  • Your gap we fillAnswering + explaining, not the voice science
  • Your team1-10 people - no spare capacity for a front desk
  • Your stageProduct built, commercial layer missing
  • Our entry pointThe receptionist, live today at $3,000/mo

How we would approach it

  • Lead with the receptionist. You need corporate leads answered before you need a new app - it is live and priced, so the first yes is small.
  • Then scope the intelligence layer: explanation, trend detection and reporting are a defined build against your stated product goals.
  • Speak your language. You talk IRB, validation and white papers - frame AI as evidence infrastructure, not a gadget.
  • Respect the founder's seat. This is a partner build under your name, not a takeover of your product.

What this is not

  • Not a rebuild of AmaTone - the voice-biomarker science is yours.
  • Not a replacement for your clinical validation work.
  • Not a consulting deck - every item above is a product we run or a build we scope.

Scope and pricing for any build are set in writing after a short scoping call. The receptionist is an existing product at published pricing; the builds are quoted per project.
