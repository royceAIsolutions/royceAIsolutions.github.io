Where to Find the Cheapest Gas in Orange County

July 15, 2026 • 4 min read

If you've filled up in OC lately, you know it hurts. Orange County gas averages $5.32/gal for regular as of July 2026. But there's a local chain that consistently undercuts everyone by 20-30¢ — Berri Brothers.

Quick answer: Berri Brothers. 14501 Red Hill Ave, Tustin. Also Mission Viejo, Sunset Beach. Regular was $5.28 as of July 2026.

Who Are Berri Brothers?

Family-owned OC company since 1976. Headquartered in Irvine. First Berri Brothers branded station opened in 2010 in Mission Viejo. They now have locations in Tustin, Mission Viejo, Sunset Beach, and more.

Why Is It So Cheap?

Most people assume cheap gas means bad gas. Not the case here. Here's why Berri Brothers is cheaper:

Is It Safe?

Yes. Every gallon sold in California must meet CARB (California Air Resources Board) standards — the same fuel regardless of the brand on the sign. Berri Brothers sources from the same California refineries as Shell and Chevron. You're getting identical gas for less.

Other Cheap Spots in OC

  • Costco — Fountain Valley, Irvine, Tustin. Usually cheapest overall but requires membership (+$60/yr). Lines can be long.
  • Berri Brothers — Tustin (Red Hill & Walnut), Mission Viejo, Sunset Beach. No membership needed.
  • Thrifty Gas — Several locations across OC. Independently priced, usually 10-15¢ below average.
  • ARCO — Multiple locations. Cash/debit only at some, but consistently cheap.

Tustin Location Tips

  • 14501 Red Hill Ave (Red Hill & Walnut)
  • 8 pumps — self-serve, pay at pump
  • Open 5 AM — 11 PM daily
  • Busy at rush hour — worth the wait (or go off-peak)
  • Phone: (714) 730-7021

What About Premium?

Berri Brothers carries regular, mid-grade, premium, and diesel. As of July 2026: Regular $5.28, Mid $5.48, Premium $5.68, Diesel $5.40. Still well under the OC average of $5.32 regular.

Bottom line: Berri Brothers is the real deal. Family-owned, 40+ years in OC, same gas as Chevron for 20-30¢ less. Royce fills up at the Tustin location regularly.
