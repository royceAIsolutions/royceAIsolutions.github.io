Jaguar is not making the same cars anymore. Type 00 is a complete reinvention — an ultra-luxury all-electric grand tourer with over 1,000 horsepower, a $130,000 starting price, and a design language that is deliberately polarizing. The brand walked away from the volume game to chase exclusivity, and Type 00 is the statement that says Jaguar intends to stand beside Bentley and Rolls-Royce, not BMW and Mercedes.

What you need to know

  • Jaguar Type 00 is the relaunch vehicle — a do-or-die all-electric grand tourer signaling Jaguar's reinvention as an ultra-luxury EV brand.
  • Confirmed: $130,000 base price (Car and Driver), over 1,000 hp expected, US deliveries expected late 2026 / early 2027, reveal window delayed to mid-late 2026.
  • Type 00 rides on a Jaguar-dedicated bespoke EV architecture, not shared with Land Rover.
  • Design is radically avant-garde: bold colors (hot pink, Miami blue in concepts), long hood, fastback rear, minimalist materials-forward interior.
  • Positioning: directly against Bentley EV, Rolls-Royce Spectre, and high-end Mercedes EQ — Jaguar is done competing with BMW and Mercedes.

Why the reinvention matters

Jaguar spent two decades competing in the premium mainstream — chasing BMW, Mercedes, and Audi with sedans and SUVs that were good but never dominant. The result was a brand that was respected but not coveted. The new strategy is the opposite: walk away from the volume game entirely and build a small number of ultra-luxury electric cars that people desire precisely because they're rare. Type 00 is the first move. It's designed to polarize — the bold colors, the theatrical proportions, the minimalist interior. Jaguar would rather be talked about than ignored.

What Type 00 delivers

On paper, Type 00 is an event car: over 1,000 horsepower, a bespoke EV platform, a $130,000 starting price, and a fastback grand tourer silhouette with a long hood that echoes the brand's greatest designs. The interior is deliberately reductive — materials-forward, minimal screens, the kind of cabin that rewards touch and time. It's not built to chase numbers in a spec sheet battle; it's built to make a statement every time the door opens. The reveal timeline keeps slipping (now mid-to-late 2026), which only adds to the anticipation.

What it means for Jaguar fans

For longtime Jaguar fans, this is the hardest and bravest moment in the brand's history. The E-Type heritage, the Le Mans wins, the cat-and-leaper identity — all of it is being compressed into a new definition of luxury. Some fans will hate it. Others will recognize that Jaguar had no future in the middle of the market, and that a bold, expensive, electric statement is the only way the leaper survives. Type 00 is a bet on the brand's soul — and the most fascinating story in luxury cars right now.
