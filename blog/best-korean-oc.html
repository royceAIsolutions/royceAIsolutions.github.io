Best Korean Restaurants in Orange County

July 15, 2026 • 5 min read

Orange County has some of the best Korean food in Southern California — from premium Korean BBQ in Irvine to old-school joints in Garden Grove and Buena Park. Here's the breakdown.

🥇 Korean BBQ (Grill-at-Table)

Baekjeong — Irvine

📍 Culver Plaza, Irvine • ⭐ 4.5 (6k+ reviews) • $$$

The gold standard. The famous Kang Ho Dong Baekjeong chain — premium beef, banchan that keeps coming, and they actually know how to cook the meat properly. Get the beef combo (brisket, ribeye, short rib). The cheese corn and egg soufflé on the grill are mandatory.

Gangnam Station — Buena Park

📍 Buena Park Downtown • ⭐ 4.5 (6k+ reviews) • $$$

Huge spot, great for groups. All-you-can-eat options available. High quality meat, solid banchan, and they have the self-service salad bar. Best for when you want to go all-out on AYCE.

I Can Barbecue Korean Grill

📍 Multiple locations • ⭐ 4.5 • $$

AYE KBBQ with great value. Multiple OC locations. The marinated beef and pork belly are the moves. Good for casual KBBQ without breaking the bank.

Gooyi92 — Irvine

📍 Irvine • ⭐ Newer spot • $$$

Upscale Korean BBQ specializing in high-end cuts. One of the newer spots in Irvine getting a lot of buzz. Great for a nicer night out.

🥈 Soups & Stews (Gukbap / Jjigae)

Jinsol Gukbap — Buena Park

📍 Buena Park • ⭐ 4.3

Famous for their pork soup (dwaeji gukbap). Rich, milky broth with tender pork. Great hangover food. Authentic Busan-style soup.

Jang Mo Jip — Irvine

📍 Irvine

Known for kimchi jjigae and homestyle Korean cooking. The kind of place where the banchan alone is worth the visit. No-frills, just good food.

Mo Ran Gak — Garden Grove

📍 Garden Grove

Institution in Garden Grove's Korean Koreatown. Known for traditional Korean dishes — naengmyeon (cold noodles), galbi jjim (braised short ribs), and samgyetang (ginseng chicken soup).

🥉 Other Favorites

Han Yang — Garden Grove

📍 Garden Grove

Old-school spot on Garden Grove Blvd in the heart of Koreatown. Known for hearty stews, soon tofu, and Korean-style fried chicken. The real deal.

Gaenali Bon Ga — Buena Park

📍 Buena Park

Eater-approved essential. Authentic homestyle Korean cooking — the banchan spread is impressive. Known for japchae, bulgogi, and seafood pancakes.

Kaju Tofu & Soon Dubu

📍 Multiple OC locations

When you want soon tofu on a cold day. Their kimchi soon tofu and stone pot bibimbap are solid. Fast, reliable, everywhere in OC.

Royce's take: For KBBQ, Baekjeong in Irvine is unbeatable for quality. For soup on a cold day, Jinsol Gukbap in Buena Park. For the full Korean experience, hit Garden Grove Blvd between Brookhurst and Euclid — that's OC's Koreatown strip.
