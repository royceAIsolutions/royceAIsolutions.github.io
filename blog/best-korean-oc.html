Best Korean Restaurants in Orange County (2026)

Updated July 15, 2026 • 6 min read

Orange County has some of the best Korean food outside of Korea. Here are the top spots ranked with ratings and must-order dishes.

Top pick: Baekjeong in Buena Park — premium Korean BBQ experience. 4.5 stars on Yelp.

1. Baekjeong (Buena Park)

Yelp: ⭐ 4.5/5 — View on Yelp

Top 3 Entrees:

  • Pork Belly (Samgyeopsal) — thick-cut, grilled table-side
  • Marinated Short Rib (LA Kalbi) — sweet, tender, signature dish
  • Beef Brislet — thin-sliced, cooks fast, perfect with ssam

The best Korean BBQ in OC. Premium meats, excellent banchan, great service. Expect a wait on weekends.

2. Gangnam Station (Irvine)

Yelp: ⭐ 4.0/5 — View on Yelp

Top 3 Entrees:

  • Beef Galbi — sweet marinated short rib
  • Spicy Pork Bulgogi — bold, flavorful
  • Seafood Pancake (Haemul Pajeon) — crispy, great starter

All-you-can-eat KBBQ with good quality for the price. Popular with students and families. Lunch specials available.

3. Jinsol Gukbap (Buena Park — Irvine location closed)

Note: The Irvine location has closed. They now operate in Buena Park (7446 Orangethorpe Ave) and Koreatown LA. The soup is worth the drive.

Yelp: ⭐ 4.0/5 — Buena Park on Yelp

Top 3 Entrees:

  • Dwaeji Gukbap (Pork Soup) — rich, comforting broth
  • Soondoobu Jjigae (Soft Tofu Stew) — spicy, silky, perfect with rice
  • Bossam (Boiled Pork Wrap) — tender pork, great with kimchi

Buena Park: 7446 Orangethorpe Ave. Koreatown: 4031 W 3rd St, LA. Both open 7 AM - 11 PM.
