Card Market Watch: Kevin Durant Rookie

July 15, 2026 · 3 min read

The 2007 Topps Kevin Durant #112 rookie card graded PSA 10 GEM MT is currently valued between $1,000 and $4,000, making it one of the most valuable modern basketball rookie cards.

Card: 2007 Topps #112 Kevin Durant RC
Grade: PSA 10 GEM MT
Cert: 47314255
Set #: 2
Est. Value: $1,000-4,000

Why It's Valuable

Kevin Durant is a future Hall of Famer, top 15 all-time scorer, and 2x Finals MVP. His true rookie card from the flagship Topps set in gem mint condition is increasingly scarce as collectors hold long-term.

Market Trends

PSA 10 population for this card is limited. As Durant's legacy solidifies, demand for high-grade rookies continues to rise. Recent eBay sales show PSA 10s trading in the $1,000-4,000 range depending on centering and eye appeal.
