How the score is built

CriterionWeightWhat it measures
Soup / broth0.20Flavour and volume — the reason to order XLB at all
Wrapper & filling0.18Skin thinness and tear resistance; filling seasoning
Craft0.15Fold consistency, soup retention, made to order
Specialization0.10Is XLB the house specialty, or one line on a big menu
Value0.12DERIVED from $/dumpling — never a hand-set score. Excluded when no price is published
Reviews0.10Rating scaled by review volume — 14 reviews is not a verdict
Access0.08Hours, wait, takeout
Proximity0.05DERIVED from driving distance to Irvine
Health0.02Sodium / richness tail — cannot flip a ranking

What this page will not do

It will not quote a price it did not read. 7 of 12 venues publish no XLB price online — their value weight is renormalised away, which is why they can rank high on craft and still be marked "price not published". It will not treat a thin review base as a verdict: review score is scaled by volume, so a 4.5 on 14 reviews scores like an unknown, not like a 4.5. Ratings and prices were read live on 2026-10-30 and are asking prices, not what you will pay after tax and tip. Always call ahead — soup dumpling counters close early when the dough runs out.
